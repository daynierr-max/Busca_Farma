## 2024-05-20 - Navigation Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in the main navigation and search inputs often lack accessible labels, making the app difficult for screen reader users to navigate.
**Action:** Always add `aria-label` to icon-only buttons and `aria-hidden="true"` to the decorative icons inside them.
